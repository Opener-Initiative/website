DECT NR+ was presented at the joint event of the Ariel OS and RIOT communities
in September [as announced previously](../../08/dect-nr-plus-riot-summit-grenoble-2026/).

If you missed the event, or want to revisit certain parts, slides and recordings are now available for 

- ***DECT NR+: License-free 5G networking*** ([video](https://makertube.net/w/5cXcnrux3x9LicpYEACbuy), [slides](https://summit.riot-os.org/2026/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2026/09/Day1-DECT-NR.pdf)) and
- ***Using DECT NR+*** ([video](https://makertube.net/w/m4F89dj2Gr81XwXSL3dngM), [slides](https://ariel-os.org/1ad/slides/1AD-4-1-hophop.pdf)).

The first talk was given at the RIOT Summit and serves as an introduction to DECT NR+. The second, held at the Ariel OS Community Day, builds on it and focuses on applications.

The audience was largely technical and familiar with competing technologies such as WiFi, 802.15.4, and LoRa; consequently, discussion during Q&A and in the hallways focused on topics such as performance characteristics (achievable range, latency, bandwidth, and the corresponding power consumption), silicon footprint of the PHY, regulatory aspects, timelines of the OPENER Initiative and the announced Last Mile Semiconductor chip, as well as the integration with existing IP-based applications.
