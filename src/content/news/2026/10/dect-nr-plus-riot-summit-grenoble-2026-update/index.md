DECT NR+ was presented in the joint event of the Ariel OS and RIOT communities
in September [as reported](../../08/dect-nr-plus-riot-summit-grenoble-2026/).

If you missed the event, or want to revisit certain parts,
recordings are available now for
[the introduction to DECT NR+ ("DECT NR+: License-free 5G networking")](https://makertube.net/w/5cXcnrux3x9LicpYEACbuy),
as well as [the applications' talk ("Using DECT NR+")](https://makertube.net/w/m4F89dj2Gr81XwXSL3dngM?start=10m20s) that builds on it.
Presentation slides are available for both ["DECT NR+: License-free 5G networking"](http://summit.riot-os.org/2026/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2026/09/Day1-DECT-NR.pdf)
and ["Using DECT NR+"](https://ariel-os.org/1ad/slides/1AD-4-1-hophop.pdf).

The audience was largely technical, and familiar with competing technologies such as WiFi, 802.15.4 and LoRa;
consequently, discussion during Q&A and in the hallways focused on topics such as
performance characteristics (achievable range, latency, bandwidth, and the corresponding power consumption),
silicon footprint of the PHY,
regulatory aspects,
timelines of OPENER Initative and the announced Last Mile Semiconductor chip,
as well the integration with existing IP-based applications.
