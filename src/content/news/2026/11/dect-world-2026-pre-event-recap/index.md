## Getting Started with NR+: Recap of a Full Day in Munich

**One day. Six stacks. A community exploring DECT NR+ together.**

On November 2, ahead of DECT World 2026, participants from Europe and beyond gathered in Munich for "Getting Started with NR+". A packed room set the tone for a day of technical exchange and hands-on exploration.

### Making NR+ Accessible

The OPENER Initiative organized the pre-event together with the [DECT Forum](https://www.dect.org). We designed the event to be accessible and informal, giving newcomers and experienced developers space to exchange ideas and expertise and explore questions that rarely fit into a conference program. Anyone interested in learning about, testing, building with, or applying DECT NR+ was welcome.

### From Talks to Toolchains

A series of introductory talks set the stage for the hands-on sessions. Roel Ottink (DECT Forum) and Prof. Dr. Mª Dolores Pérez Guirao (OPENER Initiative/[Ostfalia Hochschule für Angewandte Wissenschaften](/members/ostfalia/)) welcomed participants. Andreas Wilzeck provided an overview of DECT NR+, and stack and hardware providers introduced the ecosystem and the technologies available to explore.

After lunch, the Experience Area opened for two hands-on blocks. Teams at six islands offered guided demos and expert Q&A. Participants could work on their own laptops and log in to Raspberry Pis with a preinstalled toolchain. We ended the day with informal networking over pizza and beer.

### Six islands, one stack each

Each island presented one stack, and participants moved from station to station. STRATUM 9 showed its NR+ stack with a focus on a deterministic MAC layer for industrial use. DSR presented its OneNR+ software stack, including a demo of Matter over DECT NR+. UPCT/Qartia showed a complete stack, from custom miniature hardware and a high-performance MAC layer to IPv6 routing, real-time video streaming, and mesh networking. At the OPENER Initiative island, [Codium Electronique](/members/codium/) ran the Opener stack on its DECT NR+ hardware, and [Deveritec](/members/deveritec/) brought its dectnrPi Raspberry Pi HAT and a DECT NR+ Wireshark sniffer. Participants could bring their own code and debug it on the hardware. The islands filled up right after the talks, and the teams had their hands full answering questions. Participants could walk from demo to demo, and everything ran smoothly.

### Six Islands, Six Stacks

Each island showcased a different stack, allowing participants to explore the NR+ ecosystem firsthand.

**STRATUM 9** presented its NR+ stack with a focus on a deterministic MAC layer for industrial use.

**DSR Corporation** presented its OneNR+ software stack, including a demonstration of Matter over DECT NR+.

**UPCT (Polytechnic University of Cartagena)/ QARTIA Smart Technologies** showcased a complete stack, from custom miniature hardware and a high-performance MAC layer to IPv6 routing, real-time video streaming, and mesh networking.

**hophop** demonstrated example applications running DECT NR+ firmware on Ariel OS and connecting embedded devices and PCs over IP via DECT NR+. Participants could exchange network traffic between PCs, access the devices' applications and DECT configuration, extend those applications, and monitor traffic in Wireshark using hophop's sniffer application.

**Manulytica** presented its DECT NR+ firmware library for Zephyr RTOS, designed for low-latency networking in industrial, robotics, and machinery settings. Participants tested the library using an interactive management console and shell.

**OPENER Initiative-team:** [Codium Electronique](/members/codium/) ran the Opener stack on its DECT NR+ hardware, while [Deveritec](/members/deveritec/) brought its dectnrPi Raspberry Pi HAT and a DECT NR+ Wireshark sniffer. Participants could bring their own code and debug it on the hardware.

### The People Behind NR+

Participants asked questions, shared ideas, and exchanged expertise throughout the day. They expressed appreciation for the opportunity and the organization of the event. As Wienke Giezeman, CEO and Co-Founder of The Things Industries, put it: "Cool people doing cool things."

Feedback highlighted the value of meeting the people behind NR+, from the wider community to the companies developing the technology. Respondents described gaining a clearer understanding of where NR+ stands and how industry uses it. They also highlighted hands-on experience with real hardware and the breadth of the NR+ developer ecosystem.

Our island teams and speakers valued the preparation and collaboration and expressed willingness to support another event. Karoline Kopka of STRATUM 9 called the day "an excellent kick-off to a potential event series."

### What the Community Wants Next

Among the responses, technical deep dives and hands-on workshops were the most requested formats, followed by use case sessions and an online follow-up. Island teams and speakers also expressed interest in a joint interoperability test session.

This feedback provides a starting point for future activities.
