You're invited to a free hands-on afternoon and evening designed to give you a practical entry point into the DECT NR+ ecosystem. Join us before DECT World kicks off, for an event organized by the OPENER Initiative and hosted by the DECT Forum.

**When:** November 2, 2026 · 12:00–24:00  
**Where:** Hotel Eurostars Grand Central, Munich  
**Admission:** Free entry

**As of October 26, the event is fully booked and registration is closed. You can still register for the DECT World 2026 main event (November 3–4) on the DECT Forum website.**

[**Register for DECT World 2026**](https://www.dect.org/events/dect-world-2026/)

*Please note that the Getting Started Session is a pre-event to DECT World 2026 (November 3–4). The registration form covers both events, and attending the pre-event requires extra confirmation in the form.*

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to [hello@opener-initiative.org](mailto:hello@opener-initiative.org).

We are looking forward to seeing you in Munich.

## What to expect

- What DECT NR+ is and why it matters — no prior knowledge needed
- Hands-on experience with NR+ stacks and tools — and the chance to ask the questions you haven't been able to ask elsewhere
- Meet the OPENER Initiative team and others from the ecosystem
- Networking, pizza, and cold beer to close out the evening
