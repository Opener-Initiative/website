You're invited to a free hands-on afternoon and evening designed to give you a practical entry point into the DECT NR+ ecosystem. Join us before DECT World kicks off — organized by the OPENER Initiative and hosted by the DECT Forum.

**When:** November 2, 2026 · 12:00–24:00  
**Where:** Hotel Eurostars Grand Central, Munich  
**Admission:** Free entry

[**Register on the DECT Forum website**](https://www.dect.org/events/dect-world-2026/)

*Please note that the Getting Started Session is a pre-event to DECT World 2026 (November 3–4). The registration form covers both events, and attending the pre-event requires extra confirmation in the form.*

## What to Expect

- What DECT NR+ is and why it matters — no prior knowledge needed
- Hands-on experience with NR+ stacks and tools — and the chance to ask the questions you haven't been able to ask elsewhere
- Meet the OPENER Initiative team and others from the ecosystem
- Networking, pizza, and cold beer to close out the evening

## Preliminary Agenda

| Time\* | Session |
| --- | --- |

| 12:30 PM | Overview of DECT NR+ |
| 01:30 PM | The NR+ ecosystem: software stacks and hardware platforms |
| 02:30 PM | NR+ Experience Area: hands-on zones, guided demos, expert Q&A |
| 08:00 PM | Networking, pizza, and cold beer |
