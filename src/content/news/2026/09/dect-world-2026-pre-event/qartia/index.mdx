### QARTIA Smart Technologies

[QARTIA Smart Technologies](https://qartia.com/en/) will present its DECT NR+ technology for dual-use applications across civilian and defense environments. Delivered by the QARTIA team, the talk will cover the complete stack from custom miniature hardware and a high-performance MAC layer to IPv6 routing, real-time video streaming and resilient mesh networking.

Drawing on practical examples, the presentation will explore low-latency command and control alongside live video transmission, predictive maintenance through vibration analysis, environmental monitoring, and self-healing mesh networks. It will also introduce Stratoscanner, QARTIA's DECT NR+ spectrum analyser, and an Ethernet/DECT NR+ router for connecting private networks to the Internet.

> Our research and development focus on advancing DECT NR+ as a foundation for dual-use technologies, addressing shared challenges in industry and defense through reliable connectivity, distributed sensing and resilient communications.
>
> --- Antonio Javier García Sánchez, QARTIA Smart Technologies
