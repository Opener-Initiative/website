### Codium Electronique <OpenerBadge />

[Codium Electronique](https://codium.fr/en) will demonstrate how to use the Opener Initiative stack on its line of 
hardware products for DECT NR+ development, featuring their NR+ gateway providing DECT NR+, 
Ethernet, WiFi and LTE connectivity, and an NR+ sensor node.

Demonstrations will include how to use the development environment and integrate the
Opener stack on hardware, via two sample apps: the Opener ping-pong sample, and a
practical example of sending temperature measurements between a sensor node and a gateway.

> We are convinced DECT NR+ is the future of smart cities and high-density IoT networks. Our
> goal is to accelerate its adoption through an easy-to-use, open-source reference stack under
> the Opener Initiative umbrella.
>
> --- Axel Moinet, Codium Electronique
