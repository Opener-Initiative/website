### Deveritec <OpenerBadge />

Deveritec will demonstrate the current state of the OPENER stack on our new Raspberry Pi HAT, the dectnrPi. Visitors can bring their code and debug it together with our engineers directly on the hardware. The demos include:

* OPENER ping-pong sample: the stack running end to end on our hardware.
* DECT NR+ Wireshark sniffer: a sniffer based on the OPENER project for capturing and analysing NR+ traffic.

Beyond the current stack, we will show a pager demo that offers an outlook on what the OPENER stack and the dectnrPi could enable in the future.
