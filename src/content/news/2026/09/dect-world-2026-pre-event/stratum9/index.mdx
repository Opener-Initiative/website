### STRATUM 9

STRATUM 9 will present their NR+ Stack with focus on its deterministic MAC layer for industrial use, as well as the STRATUM 9 NR+ Starter Kit, a complete development ecosystem on the nRF9151DK / nRF9151 SMA DK.

> In industrial automation, data transmission is often time-critical and safety-related. Timing can only be promised by the layer that assigns airtime. So at STRATUM 9 we didn't tune an existing MAC, we wrote our own deterministic one: the base station owns the schedule, every mobile device knows its slots, and nothing on the channel is left to chance.
>
> --- Fredi della Torre, STRATUM 9

In a guided demo, STRATUM 9 founders Fredi della Torre and Hubert Feurstein will show you how to easily configure, test and optimize your NR+ network with the Starter Kit, how to access system diagnostics and have your own demo up and running within just a few minutes. You will be able to experiment with the Kit yourself as well.

> A development kit gives you a radio link. The Starter Kit gives you a network you can configure, measure and explain: scheduling plans, live RF and MAC statistics, your own application data over the air, and an AI assistant that reads the device state for you.
>
> --- Hubert Feurstein, STRATUM 9
