deveritec is a deep-tech engineering and Electronic Manufacturing Services (EMS) partner based in Dresden, Germany, turning customer ideas into market-ready electronics, from first concept through certification to volume production.

As the exclusive partner of [**Last Mile Semiconductor**](https://www.lm-semi.com), deveritec provides the complete DECT NR+ ecosystem:

- **High-performance boards** for long-range, robust communication in demanding IoT environments
- **Development kits** for rapid prototyping and evaluation
- **Software stack integration**, combining communication stacks, firmware, and application software
- **Expert engineering support**, from system design through integration, testing, and production readiness

Founded in 2007, deveritec has growing team of 50+, is ISO 9001:2015 certified and has in-house production and manufacturing. Beyond DECT NR+, the company's fields of competence span optoelectronics, quantum and photonic computing electronics, digital FPGA/ASIC design, and Matter-compliant device development.
