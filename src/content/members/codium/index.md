Codium Electronique is a subcontractor specialised in R&D and manufacturing of electronic products, we guide our customers from design through to the industrialisation and volume production of their innovative products. Our strength: proximity, flexibility and production capacity.

We work and experiment on DECT NR+ for over 2 years: after first tests on our own stack and exchanges with other actors, we decided to join our forces with the Opener Initiative for an open and standard compliant DECT NR+ reference stack everyone can use.

Benefiting from 12+ years of experience on wireless IoT protocols and technologies in various industries as well as academic research, our engineers participate actively in the development of the Opener stack.

Founded in 2020, Codium is an industrial SME with a human scale specialized in IoT, from small bluetooth tags to custom multi-protocol gateways and embedded Linux devices with touchscreen based IHMs: we offer a complete range of embedded electronics solutions for our customers.

## Electronic Design Office

Design, development and industrialisation of your electronic boards. From concept to certified prototype, our specialist engineers support you at every stage.

- Schematic design and PCB routing
- Firmware and software
- Prototyping and testing
- CE / FCC / RoHS certifications
- Industrialization
- IoT, embedded, wireless communications

## PCB Manufacturing

Complete assembly line for local, quality manufacturing with short lead times. Medium series from 50 to 10,000 boards/year.

- SMD and through-hole assembly
- AOI, ICT and functional testing
- Box integration and wiring
- Packaging and shipping
- Component sourcing and management
